**Parent:** #32

## Problem

The language is gaining more categories of names:
- modules;
- types;
- ordinary values;
- basis functions;
- static functions;
- operation descriptions;
- meanings.

Current shadowing rules were designed for a much smaller finite grammar. Extending them mechanically risks confusing cases where a local value hides a callable/static declaration or where type/meaning names collide.

## Decide

- namespace partitioning, at minimum value/type/module and any additional static namespace;
- whether declaration kinds share one value namespace;
- shadowing priority;
- whether a moved local continues to shadow static/ordinary declarations;
- qualification syntax;
- import collision rules;
- reserved names for built-ins and future syntax;
- diagnostic policy for ambiguous resolution.

## Non-goal

This issue need not add aliases, glob imports, reexports, or relative imports.

## Acceptance

Name resolution remains deterministic and explainable after `const fn`, `Meaning`, `Op`, sized types, and register-view syntax are added.

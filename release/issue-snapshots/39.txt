## Problem

The exact/approximate semantic boundary requires a corresponding lexical and grammar decision. Core `.qli` should not drift into floating-point semantic literals by accident.

## Proposed principle

Core semantic literals are exact.

For phases, choose a canonical exact representation, likely based on dyadic turns:

```text
j / 2^k  mod 1
```

with meaning `exp(2*pi*i*j/2^k)`.

## Decide

- surface spelling for exact phases;
- whether rational/static numeric literals are general expressions or phase-specific syntax;
- normalization of signs and modulo-one equivalence;
- allowed denominator/profile bounds;
- whether decimal floating-point literals are forbidden in quantum core;
- relationship to future approximate/backend configuration syntax.

## Acceptance

- exact `pi/8`-class phases are writable without floats;
- lexical equality/normalization is deterministic;
- wrong sign/power remains phase-sensitive;
- unsupported exact domains fail explicitly rather than approximating silently.

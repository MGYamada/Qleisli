## Decision

Qleisli 0.3.0 does **not** use Rust method-resolution magic to select quantum access.

For live `Q<T>`:

- no implicit autoref;
- no implicit quantum autoderef;
- no `Deref` / `DerefMut` path that changes or forwards quantum access authority;
- no receiver coercion that inserts `excl` or `ctrl`;
- no overload resolution between physically different access contracts.

The compiler never guesses a quantum access mode.

## Canonical 0.3.0 form

Access-sensitive quantum operations are ordinary function calls with explicit access arguments:

```qli
h(excl q);
controlled_u(ctrl control, excl target);
```

not an implicitly adjusted call such as:

```qli
q.h();
target.controlled_u(control);
```

when method resolution would need to choose a quantum access contract.

Method syntax may remain available for ordinary classical/static values or for future quantum APIs whose receiver semantics are proven not to hide an access distinction. It is not part of the 0.3.0 quantum-access core.

## Wrappers / newtypes

A wrapper around `Q<T>` cannot expose the contained live owner through ordinary `Deref` / `DerefMut` or silently forward `excl` / `ctrl`.

Any quantum-place projection must be explicit and preserve the same ownership/access checks as direct access.

## Diagnostics

```text
error: quantum access cannot be inferred through method receiver adjustment

help: call the operation explicitly with 'excl ...' or 'ctrl ...'
```

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No quantum autoref inserts `excl` or `ctrl`.
- [ ] No quantum autoderef/`DerefMut` changes an access footprint.
- [ ] Access-sensitive quantum method sugar is absent from the 0.3.0 core.
- [ ] Wrappers cannot silently forward quantum access.
- [ ] Ordinary classical/static method syntax remains unaffected where its semantics are ordinary.
- [ ] Diagnostics expose the required quantum access contract.
- [ ] Explicit-call lowering is verifier-equivalent to the declared access semantics.

## Compact rule

> **Quantum access is written, never inferred by receiver magic.**

## Problem

Approximate synthesis cost generally depends on the requested precision.

Conceptually:

```text
synthesize(U, epsilon)
```

may require increasingly large circuits as `epsilon` decreases.

If the approximation budget remains runtime-dependent, unresolved, symbolic without a finite admissible range, or otherwise open at the point of target certification, Qleisli cannot necessarily compute a concrete finite target resource bound.

Therefore an exact source meaning plus a target name is not enough to issue even a bounded *engineering* target-resource certificate for approximate synthesis. Constitutional target RS additionally requires a separately admitted exact realization and checked resource evidence (#311).

## Required boundary

Before concrete target resource certification, the following must be closed:

```text
exact source Meaning
+ selected target/profile
+ selected implementation/synthesis path
+ approximation metric
+ finite approximation budget epsilon
+ relevant composition budget
```

or an equivalent finite symbolic specialization accepted by the resource model.

The approximation contract must be part of the resource-certificate identity.

## Runtime precision

The initial 0.3.0 rule should be conservative:

> **Runtime-chosen or unbounded precision may not drive target synthesis under a closed engineering target-resource certificate.**

If an algorithm accepts a runtime accuracy request, one of the following must happen before issuing that engineering target-resource certificate:

- it is specialized to a closed finite precision;
- a declared finite upper/lower precision range yields a checked worst-case bound;
- the adaptive host process remains outside the claimed certificate scope.

## Search time vs emitted-circuit cost

Compiler/search cost remains separate from emitted quantum execution resources unless the declared resource model explicitly includes it. A quantum execution-resource claim does not by itself certify synthesis-search time.

However, successful target certification must produce a finite bound for the **actual emitted implementation**.

A timeout, unknown synthesis result, unresolved epsilon, or incomplete search cannot issue an engineering target-resource certificate, let alone constitutional target RS evidence.

## Composition

When several approximate components are combined, the selected composition rule for error budgets must also be fixed before final certification.

The compiler must not silently tighten or relax epsilon after resource analysis without rechecking the resource contract.

## Controlled operations

Where coherent control makes phase relevant, the approximation metric and bound must remain compatible with #31/#122. A cheap resource estimate for an uncontrolled channel is not automatically valid for the controlled realization.

## Non-goals

This issue does not:

- choose one approximation metric for every effect class;
- require exact synthesis;
- include synthesis-search CPU time in the quantum cost vector;
- select a specific rotation-synthesis algorithm;
- forbid approximate target execution.

## Certificate separation and fail-closed admission

Edition 2026 (#311) requires **mechanically distinct claim kinds**:

1. **Engineering numerical/refinement and approximate-resource evidence** states an error metric, closed precision budget, composition assumptions and checked quantitative bound for the **actual emitted approximate artifact**. It does not establish exact equality to the source meaning.
2. **Constitutional exact PR and target-RS evidence** requires independent exact correspondence for the actual admitted artifact and checked quantitative bounds for that exact realization. Approximate evidence cannot supply either premise merely by being finite or accurate.

Every receipt, cache entry, CLI/API result and resource certificate must bind its claim kind, exact source meaning, emitted artifact identity, semantic paradigm, realization profile, numerical/error metric, finite error/precision budget, composition assumptions and resource model. Relabeling a receipt, removing an epsilon field, or accepting a tolerance comparison as exact equality never upgrades engineering evidence to constitutional acceptance.

**Required negative and positive regressions:**

- A valid finite-budget engineering resource certificate can be issued **as engineering only**; using it as constitutional PR/target RS must be rejected.
- Changed/missing metric, epsilon, artifact, realization profile or composition parameters invalidate the old certificate. Timeout and unresolved/unbounded budgets fail closed.
- Even a displayed zero numerical error or an explicitly named `epsilon = 0` does not bypass independent exact checking.
- Numerical methods may construct an exact artifact/proof; if independently checked against the actual artifact, that exact path remains admissible and is not rejected just because the search used numerical approximations.
- Uncontrolled channel/error evidence cannot silently certify a coherently controlled, phase-sensitive realization.

This is an admission/identity constraint for the existing approximation-resource problem, not a new effect or amendment of the Constitution. General certified complex numerics remain the v1+ responsibility of #310.

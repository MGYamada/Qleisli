## Status

**0.3.0 callable-model decision fixed; implementation/conformance remain open.**

Related: #44, #45, #67, #68, #196, and the later Qartesian work.

## Decision

The 0.3.0 quantum core uses:

```text
ordinary quantum fn                  first-order
compile-time operation descriptions  duplicable only under their own ordinary/static rules
compile-time higher-order builders   allowed under bounded specialization
ordinary classical closures          only over ordinary classical/compile-time values
closure capturing live Q<T>          forbidden
linear/one-shot quantum closure       deferred
```

An unrestricted callable value may not hide live linear ownership.

## Why

Ordinary closure intuition says a callable may be copied, stored, returned, invoked later, or invoked multiple times. Those assumptions are incompatible with an invisible captured live quantum owner unless the function type itself carries a linear usage theorem.

0.3.0 therefore does not infer or simulate such a theorem through ordinary closure syntax.

## Higher-order quantum programming

Higher-order manipulation of **program descriptions / typed functions** remains allowed where the value being manipulated is not a hidden live quantum owner.

Future Qartesian/Qurry work may introduce stronger typed higher-order function values. That future system must make usage/effect/access contracts explicit rather than retroactively treating ordinary 0.3.0 closures as quantum-linear.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Live `Q<T>` cannot be captured by an unrestricted closure.
- [ ] Quantum functions are first-order at the live-owner boundary.
- [ ] Compile-time/program-description higher-order composition remains available under bounded rules.
- [ ] Callable values cannot duplicate hidden owner capability.
- [ ] Diagnostics identify live-quantum capture rather than reporting a generic closure error.
- [ ] Future linear/higher-order quantum callables require a separately specified type.

## Compact rule

> **Higher-order code may carry quantum programs; ordinary closures may not carry hidden live quantum owners.**

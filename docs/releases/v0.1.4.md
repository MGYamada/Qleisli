## Scope and compatibility

- Establish the [v0.x roadmap](../v0x-roadmap.md), including concrete work
  targets through v0.1.9 and the B019-1–B019-6 maintenance boundary.
- Place operation capabilities next in the design order, building on existing
  ownership, effects, static transforms and exact semantic evidence. Separate
  operation access, effects, established auxiliary invariants and proof roles.
- Require each proposed abstraction to identify a programmer obligation it
  removes and the evidence/checker that replaces it. Generalized source and
  public APIs require a minor release and their own extension specifications.
- Preserve the six imaginary drafts, V01-C1–C6, executable V1-C1–C5, the
  no-global-dense-matrix prerequisite, and the symbolic-kernel deferral with
  no selected release. v0.1.9 is a consolidation checkpoint, not a requirement
  to complete symbolic integration or whole-compiler soundness.
- Synchronize current-version summaries, release criteria, conformance and
  operational guidance; preserve historical release/proof records.

There is no Rust/Lean source change, new accepted syntax, public API, standard
definition, capacity change or toolchain/dependency update. Rust 1.85 and
Lean/Mathlib 4.30.0 with the pinned Physlib revision remain the baseline.
The separate non-published research package retains its version 0.1.3.
No source migration is required. Roadmap targets are not implementation or
proof results.

## Publication state

The [GitHub release record](https://github.com/MGYamada/Qleisli/releases/tag/v0.1.4)
is the evidence for the annotated `v0.1.4` tag, exact release commit, required
PR/merged-main CI and verified source distribution. Publication follows those
checks under the protected-main and immutable-release-tag rules. Earlier
uncommitted candidate archives are not release assets.

The published distribution is GitHub's complete source archives. No crates.io
package or prebuilt binary is published by this release. The nested research
package and relative dependency are retained in the complete source archive;
Physlib is fetched at its locked revision rather than vendored. Source/package
validation is distinct from registry publication, generalized language support,
and whole-compiler proof. Older 0.1.3 results do not validate this release;
the local table above and hosted exact-commit record provide separate evidence.

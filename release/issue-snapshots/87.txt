## Problem

The current effect lattice distinguishes

```text
Unitary <= Isometry <= Observe
```

but the semantic content of `Observe` needs a more precise language-level account.

A measurement operation is not adequately specified by writing only

```text
Q<Bit> -> Bit
```

because different instruments can have the same classical outcome type while leaving different post-measurement quantum states.

Likewise, classical feed-forward after measurement affects ownership and branch typing.

## Questions to decide

1. Is `Observe` semantically an instrument rather than a plain function?
2. What is the result type of measurement?
3. Does measurement consume a quantum owner, retain a post-measurement owner, or support both forms explicitly?
4. How is destructive measurement represented?
5. How is nondestructive/projective measurement represented?
6. How are general POVMs/instruments represented or deferred?
7. Must classical branches after observation return the same linear ownership shape?
8. Can a measured classical bit control later unitary/isometric operations?
9. How is coherent control distinguished syntactically and semantically from classical feed-forward?
10. How are instrument meanings represented in #46?
11. What exact semantics is required in the presence of arbitrary reference systems?
12. How do repeated measurements and reset/preparation compose?

## Required distinction

Qleisli should separate:

```text
coherent control
classical control after observation
structural ownership manipulation
```

These are physically different and must not share one ambiguous surface mechanism.

## Acceptance criteria

- The Reference gives a normative measurement/instrument judgment.
- At least destructive and state-retaining measurement have unambiguous typing.
- Branch merge rules preserve linear ownership.
- Classical feed-forward is explicit and cannot be mistaken for coherent control.
- Instrument semantics are reference-safe.
- `Meaning` or a dedicated instrument-meaning family can specify observations.
- The effect lattice has a precise operational interpretation.

## Non-goals

0.3.0 need not expose arbitrary continuous-outcome POVMs or hardware noise models.

## Design intent

`Observe` should mean more than “not unitary”:

> **it should identify a precisely typed quantum instrument boundary where classical information is created.**

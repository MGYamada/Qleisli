## Problem and target

Maintainer-reported bug, targeting **0.3.0**: valid ordinary classical `Bits<N>` source is rejected wholesale by selected Raw source emission. This is an implementation gap, not a semantic prohibition, an ownership rule, or an edition change. `Bits<0>` is a copyable singleton distinct from Unit; `Bits<1>` remains distinct from Bit.

Related: #32 #250 #44 #30 #28. Do not pull #325's 0.4.0 Bit/Bits unification into 0.3.0, revive generic QFT, or change #63's alpha deferral.

## Cause and required migration

In src/frontend/sized/raw.rs, supported() rejects every ordinary Kind::Bits(_). Input/read/bind accounting and the independently implemented replay in raw/preservation.rs lack ordinary register representation; EmptyBits/PrependBit lack Raw emission/replay support. Packaged quantum Q<Bits<N>> support is separate.

Implement bounded ordinary register transport; replace obsolete refusal expectations with positive conformance and independent negative tests. Preserve exact type identity instead of coercing Bits<0> to Unit, Bits<1> to Bit, or registers to same-width tuples. Unsupported operators and exceeded bounds must continue to fail at their actual cause.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Ordinary Bits<0> supports inputs/results, empty_bits construction, helpers, aliases, copying and drop, with its exact type retained.
2. Support bounded ordinary Bits<N> through shared source checking, Raw emission and independent source-step replay, including existing empty_bits/prepend_bit operations. Preserve widths, nested product trees and element/axis order; do not special-case only N=0.
3. Preserve generic/closed-instance responsibilities, Basis/Nat substitutions, provider/source/dependency identities, eager effects and caller order; integrate classical fn rules.
4. Bind replay to the actual emitted artifact and fresh native acceptance. Wrong ordering, changed type/width, stale source, wrong classical SSA and changed computation faults must reject; valid output IR alone is insufficient.
5. Independently test N=0,1,2, all four two-bit combinations, mixed Unit/Bit/Bits products, repeated helpers and measured classical results. Preserve quantum-reference and measurement effects; ordinary copy/drop cannot copy or erase Q<Bits<N>> owners.
6. Retain existing storage/work/native/fixture limits. Reject overflow/capacity before allocation; no new maximum-size quantum cases, convenience acceptance primitive or bypass.
7. Update Reference, diagnostics, regression tests and verification/production coverage. Documentation or checker-only examples do not complete the bug.

## Constitutional impact

Edition remains 2026 under adopted QS/PR/RS/EXACT interpretations. Preserve both admitted ordinary QLV1 ownership/classical-scope guarantees and current artifact bindings. This work does not automatically establish a new interpretation, guarantee admission, source-preservation theorem, quantitative RS discharge or release approval.

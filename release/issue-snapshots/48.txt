## Goal

From 0.3.0 onward, Qleisli language development should proceed in the order:

> **static semantics / type theory → normative language specification → implementation → conformance/proof evidence**

The current repository has rich design notes, historical decisions, implementation records and proof notes. The 0.3.0 goal is to write a new reference from scratch that reads like a programming-language specification.

The authoritative language source should be a buildable **mdBook Language Reference**.

## Documentation architecture

```text

├── design/      # rationale, decisions, proposals
```

## Normative principle

> **GitHub issue → design decision → normative Reference chapter**

Issues and design notes are inputs to the specification. Once a decision is adopted, the Reference—not the issue—is authoritative for the current language.

## Formal presentation

The Reference should present the language from its static judgments rather than from implementation order.

A central judgment may take a form such as

```text
Γs ; Γ ; Δ ⊢ e : T ! ε
```

where:

- `Γs` is the static context;
- `Γ` is the unrestricted classical context;
- `Δ` is the linear quantum-ownership context;
- `ε` is the effect.

The exact judgment is adopted only after the relevant design issues are resolved.

**QS — normative/reporting impact.** Reference rules constrain intended typing, ownership, effects, exact phase, ordered interfaces, clean/dirty obligations and complete instrument semantics under QS-2026-01. Documentation must expose unsupported profiles and source/IR/runtime preservation gaps, never infer semantic authority from a name, source example, std placement or theorem title. Pure relocation/formatting has no semantic effect when the adopted rule and links to its evidence remain identical; adding or changing a normative rule requires its own feature impact record.

**PR — normative/reporting impact.** State forward correspondence between the accepted meaning and actual admitted target artifact, with gate/device, coordinate, encoding and workspace premises. Keep this distinct from synthesis completeness, a same-wire assumption or successful numerical execution. Exact phase and arbitrary-reference obligations must survive explanatory simplification. Documentation does not discharge PR-2026-01.

**Quantitative RS — normative/reporting impact.** Distinguish safe finite program/target bounds from ownership safety, exact cleanup, checker work counters and simulator limits. Describe counted peak width, clean/dirty/backend workspace, gates, measurements/feed-forward, depth/schedule, all branches and size premises where a resource claim is made. No resource certificate or production-wide RS discharge follows from a table or build check.

**Definitions, assumptions and authority.** Reference definitions are normative language rules below the Constitution and binding interpretations, and must agree with the formal boundary; they cannot amend QS/PR/RS. No trusted assumption, semantic primitive or constitutional text is added by this documentation group. Human specification review of intended reference meanings remains required, independently of Lean proof checking. The adopted one-book arrangement supersedes the older proposed docs/reference layout in #48. Retired docs-old content is not restored; historical source, proof, artifacts and notices remain in their original evidence locations/Git history.

**Production and ledger binding.** The production-boundary chapter must name the real ordinary QLV1, native-contract and dedicated hierarchy/component routes, immutable artifact/request/candidate bindings, and execution/export gaps. It must distinguish constructors/doc/proposal output from native acceptance and published 0.2.9 evidence from the unpublished alpha tree. `governance/guarantees.json` records only the two admitted ordinary-root ownership/scope guarantees plus the three broader pending obligations; current evidence/continuity checks bind those protected meanings to present Lean definitions. Describing a new theorem does not register a guarantee. Source markers, registry inventories and rendered-link checks are integrity checks, not proof of semantic adequacy or actual test execution.

**Semantic transport, proof status and dependencies.** Documentation-only reorganization needs no mathematical transport if definitions/meaning are unchanged. A changed formal representation or intended meaning must preserve applicable ledger guarantees; where representations differ this requires checked transport. A proposed protected-meaning or premise change also requires the applicable human review, which cannot waive non-weakening or substitute for proof. Rewording cannot conceal a changed premise. Keep #27 and #32 impact records linked for implemented foundation/common-frontend rules, and the distinct #28/#30 static/basis, #29 access, #31 exact/approximate and #43–#47 type/capability/Meaning/constraint duties visible. The explicitly later #197 conveniences and public meta syntax are not grounds to relabel unfinished selected 0.3.0 requirements as future. All public rules still need their promised positive/negative and proof/evidence coverage. Full supported-profile Soundness remains the separate 0.5.0 duty.

**Edition and escalation.** Edition 2026 denotes the constitutional regime; release 0.3.0 and manifest/IR/CLI schema versions are separate. Documentation may explain ordinary adopted decisions without a new ruling. A new constitutional case, disputed interpretation, changed protected scope/premise, trusted assumption or guarantee admission needs the corresponding substantive human judgment; constitutional text changes use the Constitution's amendment/rectification procedure.

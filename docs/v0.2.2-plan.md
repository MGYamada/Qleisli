## Full-profile continuation obligations

Keep the wider four independent obligations below and let actual [sized corpus](../corpus/sized/README.md) programs select bounded slices; do not postpone source experiments until all proof infrastructure is complete.

1. **Hierarchy semantics and evidence.** Complete the [declared profile](hierarchical-ir-spec.md),
   full-space isometry/unitarity, exact finite-leaf reconstruction and call,
   encoding, compute/uncompute and transformation rules. Bind QFT, controlled
   powers and the full QPE instrument to actual circuits, including residual
   targets and arbitrary references. Keep transitional Rust-leaf premises
   explicit. Serialized success flags/handles are not evidence. Share checking
   without repeat expansion or dense matrices above six-bit leaves.
2. **Production verification.** Strictly decode/encode hierarchy artifacts,
   independently request root contracts and reconstruct evidence in a fresh
   process. Connect successor APIs and finite adapters; reject capacity excess
   and unrepresentable reverse conversions. Each external schema stays disabled
   until its pinned theorem, complete artifact/provider binding, correspondence
   and mutation tests pass under the [migration gates](lean-kernel-migration.md).
3. **Sized source and ordinary components.** Complete English G020-1 grammar,
   types, ownership/effects, semantics, lowering, limits and migration before
   production source integration. Follow the [linear-size/reshape contract](size-expressions.md):
   `n+1` recursion, checked size equality, explicit axis changes and bit reversal.
   Support static natural arguments, `Bits<n>`/`CBits<m>`, ownership-preserving
   finite folds and concretized sized static-operation arguments. Preparation,
   QFT/QPE and bulk measurement are ordinary `.qli` definitions; algorithm names
   never replace checking the generated circuit. Until v0.5, add algorithms to
   corpus under [STDLIB.md](../STDLIB.md).
4. **Execution and clients.** Run checked hierarchy IR with dyadic angles,
   shared calls, fresh sampling and actual repeated-operation costs. Reuse one
   source in order finding and a finite amplitude-estimation client through
   actual classical results. Complete R14/H1–H5 and applicable compatibility/
   distribution checks. Component-proof evaluation is not the production runtime.

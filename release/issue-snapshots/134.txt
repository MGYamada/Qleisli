## Status

Constitutional design issue for **Qleisli 0.3.0**.

Related: #129 (Constitution Draft 5), #135 (guarantee ledger), #139 (jurisdictions), #149 (Guardian).

## Problem

Qleisli needs an explicit authority model that distinguishes constitutional interpretation, mathematical verification, specification, and implementation.

A frozen Lean declaration cannot replace constitutional interpretation as the language grows. Conversely, interpretation cannot make an unproved proposition into a verified theorem.

## Adopted authority model

1. **CONSTITUTION.md** defines the entrenched jurisdictions and institutional rules.
2. **Binding Guardian interpretations and the discharged guarantee ledger** apply those rules and record the accumulated minimum guarantees.
3. **Current formal obligations and their checked proofs/evidence** implement those requirements against the actual production acceptance boundary.
4. **The Language Reference** specifies admitted language behavior consistently with those requirements.
5. **Implementation, conformance tests, and examples** must conform to the applicable specification and verified boundary.

This is a normative authority model, not a ranking that lets prose overrule a failed Lean proof. Mathematical derivability is checked under the recorded proof system and assumptions. The Guardian decides whether the formalization expresses the constitutional obligation; the checker establishes that formal proposition.

## Conflict resolution

- If a current formalization omits a protected case, revise and prove the obligation; do not treat the incomplete formalization as a constitutional exemption.
- If a required proof fails, a Guardian ruling cannot certify it. Repair the implementation/proof or withhold the affected acceptance/certification.
- If a later interpretation conflicts with a discharged guarantee, clarification must preserve or strengthen the guarantee. Apply the explicit correction process; no ordinary interpretation can repeal it.
- If Reference and implementation disagree, resolve the specification/conformance defect under the constitutional requirements. Passing tests cannot redefine the language.
- Except for a valid pre-v1 rectification authorized under #129 Article IX, if constitutional text must change, use the edition-level amendment process, subject to Article IV's non-weakening protection.

## Evolving artifacts

Within an edition, the ratified constitutional text stays fixed except for a valid pre-v1 rectification authorized under #129 Article IX. Interpretations and ledger guarantees accumulate; current theorem syntax, checker implementation, and dependency graphs may evolve while preserving those guarantees.

Neither the entire transitive Lean dependency graph nor the extension of the current acceptance predicate is frozen. #135 defines preservation and production identity; compatibility continuity belongs to #137 and conformance policy.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Make the Guardian's exclusive constitutional interpretation role explicit.
- [x] Distinguish adequacy of formalization from mechanical verification of its proposition.
- [x] Define conflicts, proof gaps, and corrections without an exemption or override path.
- [x] Make the discharged ledger a binding minimum for current formal realizations.
- [x] Keep tests and implementation subordinate to the normative requirements.
- [x] Cross-reference the authority model from the Language Reference and the ratification companion maintained outside the immutable constitutional text; link both documents to the exact ratified Constitution without modifying its protected bytes.

> **The Constitution establishes jurisdiction; the Guardian interprets its application; the checker verifies the resulting formal obligations.**

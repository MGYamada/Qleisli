# Qleisli 0.2.7

Selected 2026-10-02 (Asia/Tokyo); release authorized in this chat on the same date.
Published to [crates.io](https://crates.io/crates/qleisli/0.2.7) and
[GitHub](https://github.com/MGYamada/Qleisli/releases/tag/v0.2.7) on
2026-10-03 (Asia/Tokyo). The [publication record](../../tests/fixtures/releases/v0.2.7/publication.json)
is separate from the earlier development checks and immutable tagged candidate.
Rust, lockfile, both Lean packages, Python and std qrate are synchronized.

Scope: reduce tracked docs UTF-8 bytes by approximately 55% from the 0.2.6 baseline,
retain plans and imaginary-v1, and add nine small finite translations (69 total)
from the frozen approved sources. First sources/real diagnostics, full complex
oracles and nine semantic faults are in the [session](../../corpus/authoring/v027-small/README.md).
Rust authority/edition 2026 unchanged; VM-27-29 and general theorem gates remain open.

Subsequent work adds the [VM-27 native QIRF/request/H slice](../../tests/fixtures/verification_v027/README.md)
and [test-oriented CI lanes](../../.github/ci/README.md). This does not complete
VM-27 or the medium-term #223 migration; production authority stays in Rust.
The original validation record below predates that slice; its separate
[native validation](../../tests/fixtures/verification_v027/native-validation.json) is a local
development check, not hosted CI or exact-source release validation.
The later [semantic/decoder validation](../../tests/fixtures/verification_v027/semantic-validation.json)
records original-graph/root/reference proofs, all-field decoder tests and small
whole-hierarchy/named-QPE comparisons. General analytic reader closure remains open.

Seven [issue regressions](../../tests/fixtures/issue_fixes_v027/README.md) repair
Pair/nested tuple reconstruction, required named-QPE host CI coverage, kernel
source-policy escape hatches, corpus attribution, local call diagnostics,
exclusive proposal output and host-independent manifest warnings (#207-#212, #214).
Their local Rust/Lean validation is distinct from exact-source release CI.

The original 60% target was relaxed at the user's request to avoid forcing further
cuts. [Measured reduction](../../tests/fixtures/releases/v0.2.7/documentation-reduction.json)
records the byte inventory and unchanged plan/draft hashes.
